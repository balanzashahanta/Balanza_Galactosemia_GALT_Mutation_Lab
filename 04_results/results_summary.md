# Results Summary

## Wild-Type GALT

The wild-type (WT) GALT coding sequence from **NM_000155.4** was translated using Galaxy SeqKit Translate with reading frame 1 and the standard genetic code.

- CDS length: **1,140 bp**
- Predicted protein length: **379 amino acids**
- Start codon: **ATG**
- Stop codon: **TAA**
- Reading frame: **Frame 1**
- Reference protein: **NP_000146.2**

The predicted WT protein sequence matched the accepted RefSeq GALT protein sequence.

## Documented Mutation: c.563A>G (p.Gln188Arg)

The documented mutation was reproduced by changing nucleotide **563 from A to G** in a copy of the WT CDS.

The nucleotide substitution changed the codon from **CAG to CGG**, resulting in the amino-acid substitution **glutamine (Q) to arginine (R) at position 188**.

- Mutation type: **Single-nucleotide substitution**
- Protein consequence: **Missense**
- WT protein length: **379 amino acids**
- Mutant protein length: **379 amino acids**
- Amino-acid change: **Q188R**
- Frameshift: **No**
- Premature stop codon: **No**
- Amino acids inserted/deleted: **None**

## Artificial Mutation

An artificial synonymous mutation, **c.564G>A**, was introduced into a copy of the WT CDS. This changed the codon from **CAG to CAA**, but both codons encode glutamine (Q).

The translated artificial mutant protein was identical to the WT protein.

- Mutation type: **Single-nucleotide substitution**
- Protein consequence: **Synonymous**
- Protein length: **379 amino acids**
- Amino-acid changes: **None**
- Frameshift: **No**
- Premature stop codon: **No**

## Overall Result

The documented **c.563A>G** mutation produced a single amino-acid change, **p.Gln188Arg (Q188R)**, while the artificial **c.564G>A** mutation produced no change in the predicted protein sequence. These results demonstrate how different single-nucleotide substitutions can have different effects on the resulting protein.
